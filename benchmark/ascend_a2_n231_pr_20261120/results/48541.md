## Long-input control configurations

Earlier pure-main deployments established which configurations could carry the long inputs. A 3,072 MiB DeepEP buffer failed during target-verify graph initialization. A 1,536 MiB buffer allowed startup, but its 75,008-token prefill pool was smaller than 14 of 48 archived first-turn prompts, including one with 136,239 tokens. On the larger BF16 configuration, a 180-second transfer timeout produced 20 HTTP 500 warmup responses before stopping. With the archived 1,500-second timeout, a 16,384-token prefill chunk reached a MoE matmul that requested 1.50 GiB with 1.44 GiB free. A smaller prefill chunk and lower KV capacities completed 48/48 warmups. The C48 Decode graph then returned 7 successful responses from 55 submitted requests before the device reported a GatherV2 error (507057). The C44 comparison used eager Decode.
