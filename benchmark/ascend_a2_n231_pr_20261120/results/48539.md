## Full-model four-host comparison · weighted-lpm-f2

The 4 hosts each provided eight Ascend 910B4-1 devices. Prefill and decode ran separately at TP16 per role. Profile `memory-pressure-moonsegments` stayed constant across the comparison. Source SHAs and sanitized launch arguments are in the run records. Both arms used Mooncake Ascend Direct 0.3.14, the same device binding on the initialization thread, and the same [registration of allocator spans](../probes/torch_registration_regions.py). Registration groups PyTorch views by the allocation reported by the NPU allocator. Each transfer still uses the original address and length. A separate two-pair test transferred all 234 K/V/indexer slices across distinct NPUs and verified every destination. [Transport acceptance](../samples/real-transfer-layout.json).
